The Journey

Four basecamps.
One complete business transformation.

The RTS journey is organized into four stages, each building on the last. Every stage produces documented deliverables — so your members can show their progress, and your organization can document its community impact.

01
Basecamp 1

Business X-Ray Assessment

Know exactly where your business stands before you try to move it.

A 36-question AI-scored diagnostic across Leadership, Execution, People, Process, Customer, and Profitability. Benchmarked against industry and revenue-stage peers. Prioritized gap analysis so members know what to fix first.

Chamber Value: Your organization receives a cohort aggregate showing the collective health of your member businesses — powerful data for economic development conversations and sponsor reporting.
02
Basecamp 2

Vision to Bottom Line

Connect where you're going to how you'll get there — financially.

Vision and mission clarity. SWOT/TOWS analysis. Product/service mix analysis. Value chain mapping. 3–5 SMART strategic goals with assigned KPIs and timelines. The blueprint most small businesses never build.

Chamber Value: Every member now has a documented strategic plan — the #1 predictor of business survival. Your cohort's completion rate becomes a reportable community development outcome.
03
Basecamp 3

High Performance Framework

Turn strategy into the actions that actually change the business.

Business case documentation. Current state process flows. Voice of customer analysis. Fishbone root cause identification. RACI accountability matrices. 90-day action plans executed and measured.

Chamber Value: Members bring executed plans — not just intentions — to your events. The program creates a culture of accountability and progress within your member cohort.
04
Basecamp 4 — The Summit

Bottom Line Results & Virtual COO

Measure what changed. Prove the journey was worth it.

Before-and-after KPI dashboard: Net Profit, Revenue Growth %, Operating Cash Flow, Debt-to-Revenue, and more. Updated Operational Health Score. Virtual COO monthly coaching. Final performance report delivered.

Chamber Value: Your organization receives the complete community impact report — documented financial and operational improvement across every business in the cohort.

Getting Started

From discovery to first cohort launch.

The path from first conversation to a fully launched member cohort is shorter than you think. Here's how we build it together.

1

Discovery Session

A 60-minute working session with your leadership team. We walk through the platform, review the cohort model, discuss your member demographics, and identify potential bank co-sponsors in your market. No obligation, just clarity.

2

Cohort Design

We design the inaugural cohort together — industry focus, cohort size (typically 10–20 businesses), revenue stage targeting, and program calendar. We identify the right bank sponsor and structure the three-way partnership.

3

Member Outreach

You announce the program through your existing channels. We provide co-branded marketing materials, talking points, and application support. Members self-select based on growth readiness.

4

Cohort Launch

All enrolled businesses complete their Business X-Ray Assessment. Your organization receives the cohort aggregate health report. Individual member journeys begin. You receive quarterly progress updates throughout.

5

Graduation & Impact Report

Every business completes their before-and-after performance report. Your organization receives the full community impact document — the evidence base for your economic development story, sponsor reporting, and Cohort 2 launch.
